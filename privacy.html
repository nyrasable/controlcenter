Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nyra Control Center processes information when users connect supported social media accounts or use the application's creator tools.

1. Information we may process

Depending on the permissions granted by the user, Nyra Control Center may process basic account information such as a platform user identifier, display name, avatar, account statistics, public video information and other data made available through official platform APIs.

The application may also process media files, captions, hashtags and publishing settings that users choose to submit.

2. TikTok authentication

TikTok account access is authorized through TikTok's official authentication system. Nyra Control Center does not request or store TikTok passwords.

Authentication tokens may be processed securely when necessary to provide authorized features. Access is limited to the scopes approved by the user.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to provide requested functionality, including account connection, displaying account information, preparing creator content, uploading content as drafts, publishing when authorized, and reviewing content performance.

4. Data sharing

Nyra Control Center does not sell personal data. Information may be transmitted to supported platforms when necessary to perform an action requested by the user, such as uploading content to TikTok through its official API.

5. Data retention

Data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested service, maintain security, comply with legal requirements or support legitimate application operation. Development and sandbox data may be removed when no longer needed.

6. User control and revocation

Users may revoke Nyra Control Center's access to their TikTok account through TikTok's account settings or other available authorization controls. Revoking access prevents future API access using the revoked authorization.

7. Data security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect application credentials, authorization tokens and user data. Sensitive application secrets are not intended to be exposed in public client-side code.

8. Children's privacy

Nyra Control Center is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children below the age required to use the connected services under their applicable terms.

9. Third-party platforms

Connected services such as TikTok operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Nyra Control Center is an independent application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by TikTok or ByteDance.

10. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the application, applicable law or platform requirements evolve. The effective date above indicates the latest version.

11. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be sent to the operator of Nyra Control Center through the contact information published with the application.
